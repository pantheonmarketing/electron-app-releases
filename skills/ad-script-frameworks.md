# Ad Script Frameworks

12 proven ad script frameworks for FB/IG video ads. When creating a new ad, select the framework that best fits the product/offer and target audience. Each framework includes the structure, psychology behind it, and a filled-in example.

---

## 1. Bandwagon Effect (C.R.O.W.D.)

**Best for:** Products with social proof, trending solutions, expert endorsements

### Structure
- **C — Call out the movement:** Create curiosity by calling out what relevant groups (experts, authority figures) are doing/trying/recommending that relates to your solution.
- **R — Reject the old way:** Educate the viewer on why the common solution isn't as effective. Sets up your solution as superior.
- **O — Onboard the new way:** Introduce your product/service as the superior solution.
- **W — Wave the proof:** Build trust with social proof, studies, case studies.
- **D — Direct the viewer:** CTA. Reinforce the main benefit within the CTA.

### Example (Soothe Supps — Anti-Aging Peptide)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Call out movement | HOOK: Why do anti-aging experts swear by copper peptides over collagen? Here's the truth. | Is GHK-CU just hyped up? Anti-aging experts think not |
| Reject old way | Most people spend thousands on anti-aging creams and treatments, but your body already makes a natural compound called Copper Tripeptide-1. | |
| Onboard new way | And Soothe Supps puts this powerful compound into a peptide, it works at a cellular level to: Boost collagen, Enhance skin firmness, Reduce fine lines, Accelerate skin repair | |
| Wave the proof | This isn't just another "special ingredient" that wellness influencers are hyping up. It's actually backed by clinical studies and results. | |
| Direct the viewer | You can get it at Soothe Supps right now, to look and feel younger. | Support younger-looking skin with GHK-CU |

## 2. Industry Contrarian (D.I.S.R.U.P.T.)

**Best for:** Products that challenge the status quo, underfunded solutions, myth-busting

### Structure
- **D — Declassify the secret:** Expose suppressed industry knowledge or debunk myths.
- **I — Industry failure:** Explain why mainstream solutions fail.
- **S — Solution:** Introduce your solution.
- **R — Reinforce with evidence:** Validate with studies, case studies, scientific discoveries.
- **U — Upstage the competition:** Show what makes your solution unique.
- **P — Provide proof:** Show measurable real-world adoption (social proof).
- **T — Trigger the movement:** CTA.

### Example (Soothe Supps — GHK-Cu)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |

| Declassify | HOOK: Scientists discovered this anti-aging compound in 1973, but the beauty industry kept it quiet. | The beauty industry doesn't want you to know about this... |

| Upstage | But you don't need injections because Soothe Supps offers GHK-Cu capsules. | |
| Provide proof | And people are seeing visibly firmer skin, thicker hair, and reduced fine lines in weeks. | |
| Trigger | And you can too if you go to Soothe Supps now | Look visibly younger when you check out Soothe Supps |

## 3. Listicle (C.U.R.E.)

**Best for:** Debunking failed solutions, educational content, authority positioning

### Structure
- **C — Curiosity (List Hook):** Spark intrigue, then give reason for numbered list. Don't just say "5 mistakes" — contextualize WHY they should care.
- **U — Uncover failures:** Expose why standard approaches fail (2-3 failures).
- **R — Remedy:** Introduce your solution as the answer.
- **E — Evidence + Engage:** Add credibility through social proof, then CTA with risk reversal.

| Evidence | Thousands of women are seeing smoother thighs and renewed confidence after just 12 weeks. | |

## 4. Founder (F.O.U.N.D.E.R.)

**Best for:** Founder-led brands, mission-driven products, community building, startups

### Structure
- **F — Feature the founder:** Introduce founder(s) as relatable, empathetic humans behind a great mission.
- **O — Opposing force:** Present the enemy/problem the ideal buyer faces.
- **U — Unlocking the idea:** Reveal the breakthrough moment. Frame solution as intuitive pivot.
- **N — Numbers:** Present 3-4 tangible outcomes (numbers, timelines, revenue).
- **D — Destiny:** Position current wins as just the beginning of a bigger mission.
- **E — Engage the movement:** CTA framing supporters as co-revolutionaries. Use "join" language.
- **R — (implied) Reinforce** through visuals.

### Example (Community Wolf — Safety App)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Feature | HOOK: This is Mike and he's a regular guy. This is Nick... and he's sleeping right now. They turned the largest messaging platform into a powerful safety tool. | They turned the largest messaging platform into a powerful safety tool |
| Opposing force | In South Africa, we've got more private security officers than police. Over 30 million people are using WhatsApp groups for safety, but it's a mess. Hundreds of groups, thousands of messages, zero coordination. | |
| Unlocking | So we thought, what if we could take all these WhatsApp groups and turn them into something actually useful? | |
| Numbers | In just 4 months: Mapped over 500 crimes, Helped 2,000 people get home safely, Got 6 major security companies using our system. We're on track to hit $1.2M in revenue just in South Africa. | |
| Destiny | The best part? We're just getting started. Brazil, Mexico, Colombia — they all have the same problem. And we've got the solution. | |
| Engage | So join the movement now | Comment "Wolf" to help prevent crime |

## 5. How You Can X With/out Y (S.I.M.P.L.E.)

**Best for:** Simple products, busy audiences, "no hassle" positioning

### Structure
- **S — State the escape:** Address identity + pain in one line ("How to X without Y").
- **I — Identify pain:** Mirror frustrations with specific scenarios.
- **M — Minimal solution:** Introduce product as the "1-thing fix."
- **P — Prove:** Connect features to benefits using "because → so" logic.
- **L — Lifestyle win:** Contrast past effort with desired outcome.
- **E — Escape CTA:** Urge action with identity-tailored minimalism.

### Example (Three Beacon — Tallow Balm for Dads)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| State escape | HOOK: If you're a busy dad and hate skincare routines, this is for you | Busy dads: Your 2-second skincare solution is here |
| Identify pain | As a dad, life moves fast. Between work, family, and everything in between, I rarely have time for complicated skin routines. But I couldn't keep ignoring my dry, cracked skin. | |
| Minimal solution | Which is why I started using this all-natural balm. I put it on my face in the morning, and since it's packed with grass-fed tallow it locks in hydration for the whole day. | |
| Prove | And the beeswax protects my skin from harsh weather, without feeling greasy. Plus my skin isn't always stinging from irritation anymore because the premium raw honey really soothes my skin. | |
| Lifestyle win | I love this balm because there's no fancy steps, you just apply it and boom, you're done. | |
| Escape CTA | So if you're a dad and you're tired of your dry skin but don't wanna add a whole chore to your day, go grab this tallow balm now, it's all you need. | Stop dry, itchy skin when you grab Based Balm |

## 6. Organic (P.U.R.E.)

**Best for:** Durability/performance products, day-in-the-life content, relatable everyday proof

### Structure
- **P — Problem uncovered:** Hook with raw, relatable frustration. Blunt language.
- **U — Unfiltered chaos:** Show 2-3 real scenarios where benefits come to life.
- **R — Real evidence:** Prove performance through organic discovery. Capture genuine "Aha!" moments.
- **E — Effortless freedom:** Reveal anti-hassle payoff. Contrast easy solution against struggles.

## 7. PAS — Pain Agitate Solution

**Best for:** Health/wellness, anxiety/stress products, emotional pain points

### Structure
- **P — Problem (Relatable Struggle):** Address core pain point. Use emotional vulnerability, failed solutions.
- **A — Agitate (Intensify Pain):** Explain how pain affects them on a deeper emotional level. Physical suffering, hopelessness, isolation.
- **S — Solution (Your Product):** Present product with unique mechanism, immediate relief, social backing.

### Psychological Triggers
- Problem: Emotional vulnerability ("drowning" metaphor), failed solutions ("Just breathe" backfires)
- Agitate: Loss aversion, social proof gap, body memory of panic
- Solution: Certainty anchor, identity shift ("From victim to prepared warrior")

### Example (MyLungBuddy — Breathing Trainer)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Problem | HOOK: "Just breathe" wasn't good enough advice when I had panic attacks, my lungs always felt too weak. So getting this has been a life saver. | 'Just breathe' failed me. This worked. |
| Agitate 1 | My anxiety attacks used to leave me gasping for air, feeling like I was drowning. | |
| Agitate 2 | And deep breathing alone never seemed to help that chest-tightening panic. My breathing would always feel so weak. | |
| Solution 1 | But then I saw something online I'd never heard of — a breathing trainer. | |
| Solution 2 | Unlike basic breathing exercises, MyLungBuddy has this unique dual resistance system that trains both inhale AND exhale muscles. | |
| Solution 3 | The first time I used it during an anxiety spike? The difference was immediate. Within 10 breaths, my heart slowed and my mind cleared. | |
| Solution 4 | Now when my anxiety hits, I'm always prepared. It's so easy to use and I'm not the only one who loves this — 1000s of people love it too. | Get your MyLungBuddy now — 30 day money back guarantee |

## 8. UGLY ADS (U.G.L.Y.)

**Best for:** Low-budget, scrappy brands, standing out from polished competitors, attention-grabbing

### The 3 Questions
1. **"How can I make this SIMPLER?"** — Strip complexity. Can you say it in 5 words? Delete 70% of words. Would a 10-year-old understand?
2. **"How can I show this CHEAPLY?"** — Embrace poor production. Phone camera + natural lighting. MS Paint-style graphics. Selfie video energy.
3. **"What's the OPPOSITE of competitors?"** — Sabotage polished ad tropes. Swap beauty shots for unflattering reality. Use negative emotions. Break industry rules.

### Tactics
- Replace sentences with bullet-point text cards: "ANGER JOURNAL → THERAPIST VIDS + CBT PROMPTS → CALM"
- Paper tear hooks (ripping paper to reveal message)
- Raw, unpolished selfie-style footage
- Crude arrows, neon text, MS Paint aesthetics

### Example (Switch Research — Anger Journal)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Paper hook | HOOK: Do you get angry easily? Then check this out | (Paper tear reveal) |
| Body | This journal has made it so much easier to focus on what emotions are being triggered and why. | |
| Body | Every day, I start with a therapist-led video that this journal gives me access to. | |
| Body | And then I dive into the prompts based on cognitive behavioral therapy, which helps me slow down, become more present, and truly connect with my inner self. | |
| CTA | I couldn't think of a better way to embrace your emotions and prioritize your mental well-being. | Start your journey to inner peace by clicking the link below |

## 9. Founder Objections (P.R.O.V.E.)

**Best for:** Founder-led brands facing skepticism, overcoming "impossible" narratives, B2B

### Structure
- **P — Problem (Stark Reality Hook):** Lead with undeniable, painful truth.
- **R — Reframe (Founder's Credibility):** Contrast critics with your identity.
- **O — Objection (Name the Doubt):** Voice the specific skepticism you overcame.
- **V — Victory (Proof):** Crush doubt with tangible results.
- **E — Expand (Bigger Vision):** Pivot to bigger ambition that appeals to ideal buyer.

### Example (Community Wolf — Safety App)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Problem | HOOK: They said it was impossible to fix South Africa's broken security system. They were wrong. | They Said Fixing South Africa's Broken Security System was Impossible... |
| Problem cont. | 2 million crimes go unreported every year. Why? Because people have lost faith in the system. | |
| Reframe | But we're builders... | |

| Victory | But in just 4 months, we proved them wrong. | |
| Expand | And we're just getting started. Colombia, Brazil, Mexico — all have the same problem. | |
| CTA | So comment Wolf to join the movement. | Comment "Wolf" to join the movement |

## 10. Us VS Them (S.H.O.W.)

**Best for:** Challenge/comparison videos, performance demos, competitive positioning

### Structure
- **S — Set the challenge:** Pose a high-stakes challenge. Frame it as your product vs. everyday task or alternative.
- **H — Head-to-head showdown:** Visually pit product against something. Split-screen showing both simultaneously.
- **O — Outcome & why:** Reveal winner + explain superiority. Show features that enabled the win.
- **W — Win & CTA:** Connect victory to viewer benefit. Use "if...then" logic.

### Example (ETS — Speedloader)
| Framework | Script | Text Overlay |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Set challenge | HOOK: Can I load 5 magazines before my oatmeal cooks? | Will I be able to load 5 magazines before my 2 minute oatmeal cooks? |
| Head-to-head | Ready... GO! [Split screen: Microwave counting down / Shooter loading] Boom! 5 full magazines loaded before breakfast. | |
| Outcome & why | The ETS Speedloader makes loading your mags so easy and it works with nearly every pistol magazine. Loading 10 rounds takes seconds. And you don't even have to touch the ammo. | |
| Win & CTA | So if loading your mags faster than your oatmeal cooks sounds good, grab your ETS Speedloader today. | Want to load 5 magazines in X? Grab your ETS Speedloader now |

## 11. Triple G (G.G.G.)

**Best for:** New Year/seasonal campaigns, habit-building products, health & fitness

### Structure
- **G — Goal (Tap into aspiration):** Identify the specific outcome/transformation they want. Use year-specific language for urgency.
- **G — Gap (Reveal what's missing):** Expose why previous attempts failed. Position the missing tool/knowledge.
- **G — Gains (Show the transformation):** Present product as the bridge. Stack proof that it works.

### 5 Hook Variations
1. **Goal-Question:** "[Goal outcome]? Let's change that for 2025"
2. **Gap-Reveal:** "Still failing at [goal] because you're missing this"
3. **Tool-Bridge:** "The BEST tool to achieve [goal outcome]"
4. **Habit-Gap:** "Make [goal] a habit with [missing piece]"
5. **Year-Goal:** "Your 2025 [goal] starts with fixing this gap"

### Variable Replacement
| Variable | Example 1 | Example 2 | Example 3 | Example 4 |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Goal | Better hydration | Quit bad habit | Regrow hair | Build muscle |
| Gap | Sugar-filled drinks | Wrong tools | No medical solution | Poor nutrition |
| Gains | Clean formula | Sleek alternatives | FDA-approved treatment | Protein delivery |
| Proof | Zero ingredients | Google validation | Medical disclaimers | Quality sourcing |

### Example (Gratsi Wine)
| Framework | Script |
|-----------|--------|
| Goal | "I literally gave up wine when I started eating clean" |
| Gap | "Most wines have 3 to 5 grams of sugar, which completely ruins your macros. I had to stop drinking it." |
| Gains | "Gratsi has zero grams of sugar. It's smooth, full bodied and tastes like real wine. It fits perfectly into my health routine." |

### Example (ButcherBox)
| Framework | Script |
|-----------|--------|
| Goal | "Things that'll help you crush your fitness goals this year" |
| Gap | "No matter how much effort you put into a workout routine, it all comes down to your diet and protein intake." |
| Gains | "ButcherBox — 100% grass fed beef, wild caught seafood and organic chicken, all delivered straight to your door." |

## 12. Valentines / TEASE Framework (Curiosity Loop)

**Best for:** Affordable luxury, gifting, relationship-adjacent products, deals/discounts

### Structure
- **T — Tease:** Create curiosity with impossible/surprising claim. Creates cognitive dissonance that demands explanation.
- **E — Engage:** Build story context and journey. Introduce the "secret weapon" without revealing the connection.
- **A — Advantage:** Show benefits and value. Stack: affordability, effectiveness, desirability, premium perception.
- **S — Satisfy:** Resolve the curiosity loop. The "aha!" moment that makes the initial claim logical.
- **E — Encourage:** CTA with urgency and additional incentive.

### Why It Works
Creates a logical puzzle that can only be solved by watching the entire ad → high retention and engagement.

### Hook Variations
1. "I got my boyfriend a Valentine's gift for free"
2. "My boyfriend paid for our entire Valentine's date (and he doesn't know it)"
3. "This £12 perfume saved me £200 on Valentine's Day"
4. "How I turned a £10 purchase into a free romantic dinner"
5. "The Valentine's hack that made my boyfriend pay for everything"

### Example (Essence Vault — Perfume)
| Framework | Script |
|-----------|--------|
| Tease | "I got my boyfriend a Valentine's gift for free." |
| Engage | "So I went on that fishing date with my boyfriend and you know, I needed that extra oomph. So of course I used my favorite perfume from The Essence Vault, F number 82." |
| Advantage | "I swear by this stuff. It's just so affordable and long lasting. My boyfriend was all over me. He kept asking me how much I spent on this perfume, and when I told him it was from the Essence Vault, he did not believe me." |
| Satisfy | "And then he started saying, because I'd saved so much money buying from the Essence Vault, I could pay for this date. Which I replied no." (REVEAL: The "free gift" was the date itself) |
| Encourage | "And you can now get 30% off plus a free gift worth up to £15 this Valentine's Day. Check it out." |

## Framework Selection Guide

| If the product/offer has... | Use this framework |
|---|---|
| Expert endorsements, trending status | **1. Bandwagon (CROWD)** |
| Industry secrets, myth-busting angle | **2. Industry Contrarian (DISRUPT)** |
| Multiple failed alternatives to debunk | **3. Listicle (CURE)** |
| Founder story, mission, community | **4. Founder (FOUNDER)** |
| Busy audience, simple solution | **5. How You Can X (SIMPLE)** |
| Durability/performance to prove | **6. Organic (PURE)** |
| Deep emotional pain point | **7. PAS** |
| Need to stand out, low budget | **8. UGLY ADS (UGLY)** |
| Overcoming skepticism, "impossible" claims | **9. Founder Objections (PROVE)** |
| Clear competitive advantage to demo | **10. Us VS Them (SHOW)** |
| Seasonal/New Year, habit formation | **11. Triple G (GGG)** |
| Deals, gifting, curiosity-driven | **12. TEASE (Curiosity Loop)** |
