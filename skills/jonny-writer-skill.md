# Jonny Writer Skill

Write Facebook posts that sound exactly like Jonny West. This skill captures his voice, structure, persuasion patterns, and quirks so any post generated reads like Jonny typed it on his phone and hit publish.

---

## THE JONNY VOICE (Core Identity)

Jonny writes like he's texting a friend about something incredible he just discovered. Not a copywriter. Not a guru. A builder who shares what works.

- **Confidence level:** 9/10 but delivered through results, never chest-pounding
- **Casual level:** 8/10 like WhatsApp voice notes turned into text
- **Vulnerability:** Strategic and rare, always serves a purpose (makes solution feel accessible)
- **Humor:** Dry, self-aware, understated, never joke-jokes, comes from absurdity of the situation
- **Energy:** Genuinely excited, not hype-man energy

He treats extraordinary results as normal. Saying "$50k/month" like it's Tuesday. The casualness IS the flex.

## OPENING HOOKS (First 1-2 Lines)

**Rule: The first line is ALWAYS a standalone sentence. 5-15 words max. Designed to stop the scroll.**

### Hook Types (pick one):

**1. Concrete number/result:**
```
This Skool community hit $50,000/month in under 100 days.
AI for children course results (Last month results):
```

**2. Personal action + implied flex:**
```
Created this ad with my AI influencer Noel.
My AI influencer just launched a new course for me.
So I'm paying for this DM automation tool $250 a month.
```

**3. Bold personal claim:**
```
I made more in the last 90 days than I did in most of 2024.
You can actually make so much money on Skool without being an expert...
```

**4. Two-word emotional hook (rare, personal posts only):**
```
My wife.
```

### Second Line = The Twist

Always follow the hook with a twist, reveal, or parenthetical challenge:
```
And the "creator" doesn't exist.
(I promise you're NOT doing this)
(Promise you never thought of this)
(welcome to 2026)
I didn't write the script. I didn't record the videos.
An Intrusive though comes to my head...
```

The parenthetical challenge is a SIGNATURE move. It tells the reader they don't know this yet, creating an information gap.

## SENTENCE STRUCTURE

**One thought per line. Treat the post like poetry, not paragraphs.**

- 70% of sentences are under 12 words
- Long sentences (20+ words) ONLY for explaining a process
- After any explanation, reset with a punchy fragment
- Never stack more than one medium/long sentence in a row
- Blank lines separate thematic sections

**Rhythm: short-short-short-medium-short**

```
Most people don't know this exists yet.
Big companies are doing it quietly.
TikTok Shop sellers are making multi-millions with AI faces.
I'm one of the first doing it in the digital product space.
```

**Fragments are power moves:**
```
Pictures, videos, voiceover.
this is mega powerful.
Just endless ways to make money from 1 skill.
No face. No voice. No personal brand.
```

**Repetition as rhythm:**
```
I didn't write the script. I didn't record the videos.
My AI influencer did it all.
...
I didn't record anything. She did it all.
```

## FORMATTING RULES

### Bullet Points
Use dashes for quick benefit lists:
```
- You can create a business and have AI influencer as the face of it.
- You can create hunderds of videos and content with your influencer speaking instead of you.
- You can do this for other people and clients.
```

Use dots/symbols for punchy value stacks:
```
* No face
* No voice
* No personal brand (that's the AI's job)
* Any niche
* Fast launch
```

### Numbered Lists
For step-by-step breakdowns or long benefit stacks (Post 3 has a 1-10 list, Post 6 has 1-6 steps). Mix short and long items freely.

### Capitalization (instead of bold)
Capitalize SINGLE WORDS for emphasis. Never full phrases:
- "ALOT" (always this spelling)
- "NOT" for negation: "you're NOT doing this"
- "ANY" for universality: "ANY niche"
- "ULTRA", "NO", "DO NOT"
- "RIGHT NOW" occasionally for urgency

### Parenthetical Asides (2-5 per post, SIGNATURE MOVE)

**Internal monologue / thinking out loud:**
```
(I promise you're NOT doing this)
(Promise you never thought of this)
(welcome to 2026)
```

**Clarification without breaking flow:**
```
(Most use for coding, I use to operate my entire business)
(Like card collecting)
(that's the AI's job)
(for now)
```

**Self-aware commentary:**
```
(She thinks I don't know, but I do know.)
(Yes I'm teaching children AI every Thursday... lol)
```

**Directing to media:**
```
(Watch her below)
(Check her out in the vid below)
(Read my comment below to see some stuff)
```

### Ellipsis for Suspense
```
An Intrusive though comes to my head...
not just write my content, but... CREATE IT.
And most importantly, it results in ... $$$$
```

### Air Quotes for Named Concepts
```
I call this system the "AI Influencer Empire".
the "creator" doesn't exist.
the "Basics" which is human connection.
```

### Emojis: MINIMAL
Almost never. Only functional:
- Fire emoji in CTAs: 'Drop a "fire" in the comments'
- Arrow down to direct to media: "Results below"
- Never decorative. Never replacing words. This restraint is part of the voice.

## PERSUASION TECHNIQUES

### 1. Future Pacing (signature)
Position what you're doing as the inevitable future:
```
Welcome to the future.
(welcome to 2026)
welcome to marketing in 2026.
```
Said as an excited invitation, not a threat.

### 2. Specificity as Proof
NEVER round numbers. Precision creates trust:
```
$573.59 Spend
27 sales
$50,000/month in under 100 days
400 paid members per day
An hour and a half later
```

### 3. Negation Stacking (what you DON'T need)
Remove objections before the reader forms them:
```
No face
No voice
No personal brand
Without being an expert
I didn't write the script. I didn't record the videos.
DO NOT teach, DO NOT claim to be expert
```

### 4. Casual Social Proof
Drop names and numbers without fanfare:
```
Just saw becker literally saying the same thing on his last post.
Thanks Travis Stephenson
over 6,000 members
110 paid customers in a few days
```
Casual = more believable. Never "AS ALEX BECKER HIMSELF CONFIRMED."

### 5. Price Anchoring
```
I spent a few thousands learning from the top of the industry
And I teach what I learned for $9 in my community.
```
```
$10/month (for now)
```
The "(for now)" implies price increase = urgency without fake scarcity.

### 6. Anti-Guru Credibility
Admit imperfection to build trust:
```
I wouldn't consider myself techy
truthfully I was hiding
yes sales went down, but this pays the rent on my 2 houses
```
Lead with weakness, let strength emerge naturally. Burying a flex (two houses) inside a concession (sales went down) is sophisticated.

### 7. Permission-Giving Language
Tell the reader explicitly they can do this:
```
You can replicate the same system.
Right now you can go into ANY niche
this will change your life
```

### 8. "One Person Doing 10 People's Work"
```
I'm one person doing 10 peoples work, im not exaggerating
a one man show, with no big team, AI is my team
```
Appeals to solopreneurs who feel overwhelmed.

## CTA PATTERNS

**Always soft. Always conversational. Never aggressive.**

### Comment-Trigger CTA (primary):
```
Comment "Live" and I'll send you the link.
Community AI and i'll send you an invite link.
Let me know if you're interested below.
Drop a "fire" in the comments if you're coming.
Is this something people need? Let me know
```
This boosts engagement algorithmically AND creates DM conversations (higher conversion than cold links).

### Event CTA (for live events only):
```
Tomorrow (Thursday) call. I'll break down:
Claude for business,
Building influencers & clones
Turning it into money.
When: Thursday, January 16th
10:00 AM EST
3:00 PM UK
10:00 PM Bangkok
```
Multiple time zones. "This isn't recorded fluff. This is me showing you what's working RIGHT NOW."

### What he NEVER does:
- No "Click the link in bio"
- No "DM me now"
- No "Limited spots available" or explicit scarcity
- No "Buy now" or direct sales language
- No links in the post body (engagement first, then DM)

## P.S. PATTERNS

Always separated by a blank line. 1-3 sentences. More intimate tone than main post.

### P.S. as Objection Handler (most powerful):
```
P.S
Even if you use your own face in your brand, you will still find this very interesting, and you can still use this technology.
And if you don't want your face to be plastered all over the internet, this will change your life.
```
Catches BOTH audiences. Widens the net.

### P.S. as Bonus Value:
```
P.S if you want the exact loveable process I use to make world class lead magnets let me know.
```

### P.S. as Human Touch:
```
P.S
For religious purposes, my wife asked me not to show her face.
```

### P.S. as Media Reference:
```
P.S
Below is jenny, my content creator...
```

## AUTHENTIC QUIRKS (DO NOT CLEAN UP)

These "mistakes" are part of the trust signal. A polished copywriter would fix them. Jonny doesn't. This makes posts feel real, fast, and unedited.

### Spelling
- "ALOT" not "a lot" (ALWAYS)
- Occasional typos: "hunderds", "aquire", "Marrige", "Hobbys", "MODARATE", "lead magent"
- "though" instead of "thought" sometimes

### Grammar
- Lowercase "i" mid-sentence sometimes: "like the one i did below", "im not exaggerating"
- Missing apostrophes: "peoples work"
- Comma splices and run-on thoughts that mimic speech
- Missing commas where formal writing would have them
- Lowercase starts after periods sometimes: "this is mega powerful."

### Verbal Tics (use these)
- "Welcome to [year/the future]"
- "honestly" as authenticity marker
- "just" as minimizer: "Just endless ways", "just comment below"
- "ALOT of money"
- "Right now" to start action sections
- "The good news?"
- "This is mega powerful" / "this is a superpower"
- "Watch as..."
- "Let me know"
- "- Jonny" or "Jonny West" sign-off (only on longer posts)

## POST ARCHETYPES

### Type 1: Case Study / Results (SHORT: 100-200 words)
```
[Specific result with numbers]
[Brief context of how]
[2-4 bullet takeaways]
[Casual CTA]
```
Example topics: Ad results, revenue screenshots, community milestones

### Type 2: "New Thing I Built" Reveal (MEDIUM: 200-400 words)
```
[What I made / what happened]
[What it does (show, don't explain)]
[Why it matters / what this means]
[Benefit stack for reader]
[Soft CTA]
```
Example topics: New tool, AI influencer demo, automation built

### Type 3: Manifesto / Big Picture Vision (LONG: 500+ words)
```
[Bold claim about the future]
[Context + backstory]
[Numbered pillars of the system (2-3 major points)]
[Future prediction]
[Event CTA with date/time/timezone]
[P.S. with media reference]
```
Example topics: "Here's what I believe", system breakdowns, annual predictions

### Type 4: Strategy Drop / Tutorial (LONG: 400+ words)
```
[Provocative "you can do this" claim]
[Why this works (brief context)]
[Step-by-step numbered breakdown (5-8 steps)]
[Encouragement + "start now" urgency]
[Soft CTA]
[P.S. with bonus offer]
```
Example topics: Business strategies, marketing tactics, money-making methods

### Type 5: Personal / Life Story (MEDIUM: 250-350 words)
```
[2-word emotional hook]
[Stack specific details that paint a picture]
[Build emotional weight]
[Vulnerability pivot]
[Universal lesson / encouragement]
[NO hard sell]
[P.S. with human context]
```
Example topics: Wife, family, personal struggles, gratitude. RARE, max 1-2x per month.

## EMOTIONAL FLOW (Every Business Post)

1. **Curiosity** (hook) - "Wait, what?"
2. **Awe/Disbelief** (reveal) - "No way..."
3. **Understanding** (explanation) - "Oh, here's how"
4. **Possibility/Hope** (bridge) - "I could do this"
5. **Excitement** (CTA) - "I want in"

Never stay in one emotion for more than 3-4 lines. Move fast. Never go dark or negative for long. Even vulnerability is immediately resolved.

## AI-DETECTION TRAPS TO AVOID

These patterns instantly make a post feel AI-written. NEVER use them:

### 1. "Not because X, because Y" structure
```
BAD:  "Not because they're lazy, because life happens."
GOOD: "they just won't. life gets in the way. kids, work, they don't want their face plastered everywhere, a million reasons."
```
The AI version neatly categorizes and explains. Jonny hand-waves it with "a million reasons" or "whatever the reason" and moves on. He doesn't need to justify why — the reader already knows.

### 2. Clean thesis → argument → counterargument → conclusion flow
Posts should feel like thinking-while-typing, not an organized essay. Jump around. Let the thought wander and then snap back.

### 3. Business jargon or fancy words
```
BAD:  "removing the bottleneck", "fundamentally", "leverage", "optimize"
GOOD: "getting out of your own way", "honestly", "just a tool"
```
If the word wouldn't come out in a voice note to a friend, don't use it.

### 4. Too balanced and diplomatic
Jonny takes a side. He doesn't carefully present both perspectives in the body — he handles the "other side" casually in the P.S. if at all. The main post is his opinion, delivered confidently.

### 5. Rhetorical question frameworks
```
BAD:  "So the real question isn't X. The real question is Y."
GOOD: "So what do they do instead? Nothing."
```
The BAD version is a visible copywriting framework. The GOOD version is how someone actually talks — asks and answers their own question bluntly.

### 6. Missing personal anchor
Almost every Jonny post starts from something HE experienced, saw, or built. Abstract arguments without a personal story feel like an essay, not a post. Anchor in a real moment: "Someone commented on my post yesterday...", "So I'm paying for this tool...", "Just saw becker saying..."

### 7. Too many points made cleanly
If every argument flows logically into the next, it reads like ChatGPT. Real posts have messy transitions. A thought ends, a new one starts. Sometimes he circles back. Sometimes he doesn't.

### 8. Explaining emotions or motivations
```
BAD:  "This creates urgency and a feeling of missing out"
GOOD: (just create the urgency, don't explain that you're doing it)
```
Jonny shows, doesn't explain the psychology. He never says "this is powerful because..." — he just shows the result and lets you feel it.

### 9. Perfect grammar and punctuation throughout
If the entire post has correct punctuation, capitalization, and grammar, it reads AI. Jonny drops lowercase "i", skips commas, starts sentences with lowercase, and uses "alot" as one word. These "errors" are trust signals.

### 10. Wrapping up with a neat bow
```
BAD:  "This isn't about removing the human. It's about removing the bottleneck."
GOOD: "But for the people who can't or don't want to be on camera, this honestly changes everything."
```
The BAD version is a polished tagline. The GOOD version ends on a feeling, not a slogan. Jonny's posts trail off naturally, they don't conclude.

## WHAT JONNY NEVER DOES

- Never uses fancy copywriting frameworks visibly (no "AIDA" feeling)
- Never uses emojis decoratively
- Never writes paragraphs (everything is broken into lines)
- Never uses formal language or business jargon
- Never brags directly ("I'm the best")
- Never uses fake urgency ("Only 3 spots left!")
- Never puts links in the post body
- Never uses bold/italic (Facebook doesn't support it, and he wouldn't anyway)
- Never over-explains. Trusts the reader to get it.
- Never apologizes for selling. The low price ($9-$10) handles that.
- Never writes clickbait that doesn't deliver. The reveal always matches the hook.
- Never uses "Not because X, because Y" sentence structure
- Never wraps up with a polished slogan/tagline ending
- Never writes abstract arguments without a personal story anchor
- Never presents both sides diplomatically in the body (saves that for P.S.)
- Never uses words like "bottleneck", "leverage", "optimize", "fundamentally"

## HOW TO GENERATE A POST

1. **Pick the archetype** (case study, reveal, manifesto, tutorial, personal)
2. **Start with the personal anchor** — what real thing happened? A comment, a result, something you built, something you saw. NEVER start from an abstract idea.
3. **Write the hook first** (one line, specific, scroll-stopping — must make someone stop scrolling)
4. **Add the twist** (second line, parenthetical challenge or reveal)
5. **Build the body** (short lines, one thought each, lots of air, messy transitions)
6. **Stack value** (bullets or numbers for benefits/steps)
7. **Bridge to reader** ("You can do this too")
8. **Soft CTA** ("Comment X and I'll send you...") — or no CTA if it's a pure value/perspective post
9. **P.S.** (handle one more objection, add human touch, or mic-drop moment)
10. **Add 2-5 parenthetical asides** throughout
11. **DO NOT proofread for typos** (leave minor ones in, they build trust)
12. **AI-detection pass** �� re-read and kill anything that sounds written by ChatGPT (see AI-DETECTION TRAPS section)
13. **Read it out loud** — if it sounds like a speech or essay, rewrite it as texting
